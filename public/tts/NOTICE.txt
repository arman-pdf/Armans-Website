CREDITS FOR THE OPTIONAL SPOKEN ANSWERS
=======================================

The "natural voice" is AI-generated speech. It is not a recording of a person.
It runs entirely in your browser; your questions and the answers are not sent anywhere by it.

Model
  Pocket TTS, by Kyutai (Manu Orsini, Simon Rouard, Gabriel De Marmiesse, Vaclav Volhejn,
  Neil Zeghidour, Alexandre Defossez). https://huggingface.co/kyutai/pocket-tts
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/   (full text: LICENSE-model-CC-BY-4.0.txt)

Voices
  "Marius" is a built-in voice from Kyutai's voice collection (voice-donations),
  released under CC0 (public domain dedication). https://huggingface.co/kyutai/tts-voices
  It is AI-generated speech, not a recording of the person who donated the original.

ONNX export of the model
  KevinAHM/pocket-tts-onnx, CC BY 4.0. https://huggingface.co/KevinAHM/pocket-tts-onnx
  Loaded from a pinned version and checked against its SHA-256 fingerprint when downloaded.

Changes made
  The model files are 8-bit quantised (int8) by the exporter. This site uses a single voice,
  skips the voice-cloning component, and runs the model with ONNX Runtime Web in your browser.

Code
  The in-browser speech pipeline (inference-worker.js, PCMPlayerWorklet.js, EventEmitter.js) is adapted
  from KevinAHM/pocket-tts-web, Apache License 2.0 (LICENSE-code-APACHE-2.0.txt).
  sentencepiece.js: SentencePiece for JavaScript, Apache 2.0.
  ONNX Runtime Web 1.20.0 (self-hosted in /tts/ort), MIT licence (ort/LICENSE-onnxruntime-MIT.txt).

Use of this voice must follow Kyutai's terms: no voice impersonation or cloning without consent, and
no presenting generated speech as a genuine recording of a real person.
