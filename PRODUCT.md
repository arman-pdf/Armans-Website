# Product

## What this is
Arman Kanorwalla's portfolio. Arman is a recent University of Waterloo graduate (BSc Honours Environmental Science, Geoscience specialisation) seeking environmental, groundwater, GIS and ESG roles. Mode: **Experience** (a portfolio; the work leads and the interface recedes).

## Who it is for
Recruiters and hiring managers at environmental consultancies, municipalities, mining/exploration and ESG firms. They skim for 30 seconds, then read one or two projects properly. A second audience is peers and professors.

## What it must do
- Make Arman memorable as a specific person: a geoscientist who works with real terrain, water and data.
- Keep every resume fact accurate. Copy lives in `src/content.js`; do not invent claims.
- Make contact effortless (email, LinkedIn, a working message form).
- Be fast: static-first, 3D only on capable desktops, no blocking loaders beyond a short first-visit intro.

## Voice
Plain, precise, a little dry. A field scientist writing clearly, not a brand.

## World
Field survey: topographic sheets, benchmarks, survey flagging tape, borehole logs. Real coordinates and elevations (Waterloo Moraine, 295–425 m) appear as quiet details.

## Constraints
- Non-technical owner; keep tooling simple. Hosting target: Netlify (free). Chat model: Ollama Cloud (free), text first.
- $0 running cost; nothing may bill unexpectedly.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Mobile gets a static image instead of WebGL.

## Anti-references
The previous version of this site (glass cards, particles, rainbow highlights) and generic template resumes.
